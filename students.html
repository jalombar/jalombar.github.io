For Prospective Students

My research is carried out with Allegheny College undergraduates: they set up the simulations, analyze the results, and co-author the papers. Wondering what students in my group go on to do? Charlie Gibson '25 led our study of stripped stars from high-speed collisions (Gibson et al. 2025) — work supported by an Illinois Space Grant and featured by the College — and is now a graduate student at Northwestern.

Emma Chambers '24 and Hans Vanderzyden '25 studied how a pair of orbiting black holes is affected by gas from stars that wander too close and are torn apart — encounters that send out gravitational waves detectable on Earth. Hans is now pursuing graduate studies at the Air Force Institute of Technology (AFIT), while Emma is pursuing her PhD in astrophysics at RIT.

If you are a high school student wondering whether astrophysics might be for you, or an Allegheny student looking for a research experience, I would love to hear from you at jalombar at allegheny dot edu.
